# Scope and plans

## Current Stage 1

Only basic ranger movement, a third-person camera, a primitive forest, seed collection, tree planting and a forest-health indicator are implemented. Instructions and interaction feedback support that loop.

## Limitations

- Primitive artwork, no character animations or audio.
- One small clearing with three seeds and three planting plots; no persistent progress.
- Health is a simple score based on planted trees, not an ecosystem simulation.
- HUD uses IMGUI; narrow aspect ratios and accessibility options need further work.
- Camera obstruction checks are designed for this small open clearing, not complex indoor spaces.
- Litter collection, waste reuse, bird guidance, sneaking, patrol detection and other companion abilities are not implemented in the simplified version.
- No combat, weapons, inventory screen, gamepad/mobile input or settings menu.

## Future concepts — not implemented

**Possible Stage 1 expansion:** tutorial pacing and bird scouting, if the team later chooses to expand the prototype.

**Stage 2 — Halt the Crew's Mission:** monkey companion, non-violent patrol distractions, collection and appropriate reuse of discarded materials.

**Stage 3 — Final Restoration:** larger animal helpers, abandoned machinery removal and wider restoration tasks.

Art/audio, environmental education content, accessibility, platform builds and broader user testing also remain future work. These are design ideas, not completed team contributions.
